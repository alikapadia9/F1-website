THE LOGBOOK, EXPLAINED

Every lesson works triple time.

In NSW, learners under 25 need 120 logbook hours (including 20 night hours) before their test. Under the 3-for-1 scheme, every hour of structured lessons with a licensed instructor counts as 3 logbook hours — up to 10 lesson-hours, or 30 logbook hours total. Learners 25 and over are exempt from the logbook requirement.

Rules can change — always confirm current requirements on the Transport for NSW website.
