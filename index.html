THE LOGBOOK, EXPLAINED

Every lesson works triple time.

In NSW, learners under 25 need 120 logbook hours (including 20 night hours) before their test. Under the 3-for-1 scheme, every hour of lessons with a licensed instructor like Fatema counts as 3 logbook hours — up to 10 lesson-hours, or 30 logbook hours in total. Learners 25 and over are exempt from the logbook requirement altogether.

In plain terms: 10 hours in the car with Fatema does the work of 30 hours logged any other way. That's a genuinely faster route through the paperwork.

Rules can change — always confirm current requirements on the Transport for NSW website.
